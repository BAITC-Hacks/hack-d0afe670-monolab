## Manhole dataset: de-augmentation + hazard-only mapping (2026-09-27)

### Why de-augmentation was necessary

BITS `manhole_detection-wdc0v` v1 (Roboflow YOLOv8 export) bakes augmentations into **static files** rather than applying them at train time. Each source photo appears as up to three copies sharing a stem:

`{source}_jpg.rf.{32-char-hex-hash}.jpg`

Example group (kept file marked `*`):

```
[0001_jpg]
  * 0001_jpg.rf.8d9bf3fd46e2dccf57ed4c8998d91fea.jpg
    0001_jpg.rf.96a97f64af62333cdc9cd3a2a3f59d1f.jpg
    0001_jpg.rf.ca2cac909915b8d7c3c6e4cb046f8a0d.jpg
```

If left intact, `prepare_dataset.py`'s per-source stratified split can place augmented copies of the same underlying scene into **different** train/val/test folds — inflating metrics via near-duplicate leakage. Script: `training/dedupe_roboflow_export.py` → output at `/Users/darkhan/data/roboflow_manhole_dedup/` (original download preserved at `roboflow_manhole/`).

| Stage | Images | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Raw Roboflow export | **2,673** | 823 groups × 3 aug copies + 204 singletons |
| After dedup | **1,027** | One file per `{source}_jpg` stem (filename-based grouping; 100% matched `.rf.<hash>` pattern) |

### Hazard-only mapping decision

Classes in the export: `closed manhole`, `improperly closed manhole`, `open manhole`.

**Closed manhole is normal infrastructure, not a defect.** Mapping it to `sunken_manhole` would teach the fast-track router (`HAZARD_FASTTRACK`) to flag routine manholes as emergencies.

Real-merge flags (use **deduped** path):

```bash
--extra-dataset '/Users/darkhan/data/roboflow_manhole_dedup:improperly closed manhole=sunken_manhole,open manhole=sunken_manhole'
--explicit-drop '/Users/darkhan/data/roboflow_manhole_dedup:closed manhole'
```

Dry-run v2 (`training/dataset_dryrun_manhole_rutting_v2`): **281 images kept**, **292 `sunken_manhole` instances** (down from ~803 hazard boxes and ~3,000 instances when all classes + aug copies were included). `--explicit-drop` logged `closed manhole=811`; no unmapped-class WARNING for `closed manhole`.

### `sunken_manhole` volume limitation

After dedup + hazard-only filtering, `sunken_manhole` is the **smallest and most fragile class** in the 5-class taxonomy — far below RDD crack/pothole volume and even below Saha rutting (~503 annotated images). **Dedicated KZ photo collection for open/improperly-closed manholes is the highest-priority gap** before expecting reliable local fast-track routing.

## Limitations (honest, pre-training)

1. **No KZ photos** — stage-1 KZ fine-tuning is skipped; two-stage mode falls back to training stage 1 on the full combined dataset instead of a local subset. This removes the intended domain-adaptation benefit for Kazakhstan roads.

2. **RDD2022 domain gap** — even after merge, pothole/crack classes are overwhelmingly from Japan/India/Czech/etc., not Almaty/Astana asphalt. Expect **low transfer** to local field photos until KZ labels are added.

3. **`sunken_manhole` and `rutting`** depend entirely on small external datasets (Roboflow + Saha) — likely **weakest AP50** classes due to low volume vs RDD2022's ~47K images and possible label noise in Roboflow exports. **`sunken_manhole` is especially thin** after manhole dedup + hazard-only remap (~292 instances).

4. **No local validation** — test split is stratified from the same foreign sources; it measures generalization within those distributions, **not** Kazakhstan deployment accuracy.

5. **Class imbalance** — RDD maps only D00/D10/D20/D40; `sunken_manhole` has zero native RDD labels.
